IF PLANS CHANGE

Please give
a little notice.

The published cancellation policy

  • Give at least 24 hours’ notice when cancelling.
  • With less than 24 hours’ notice, 50% of the session fee applies.
  • Same-day cancellations or no-shows are charged at 100%.
  • The original policy allows the fee to be waived if the appointment can be refilled.

Contact Caroline as early as possible. If the same-day and under-24-hour categories overlap, her published policy specifies the same-day rate. Confirm the applicable terms in the booking process.

A FEW COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you arrive.

Do I need to choose a treatment first?

No. If you’re unsure, call or email Caroline before making an appointment. She can explain the choices and discuss suitability.

Can I claim through health insurance?

Check your own policy and the practitioner’s current registration with your insurer before booking. This concept does not promise reimbursement.

Can I book a 120-minute combination?

The source website mentions longer combinations, but a 120-minute service is not present in the current booking menu. Please ask Caroline about availability and price.

Where can I see available dates?

Use the existing Acuity booking service linked from this site. Availability, appointment details and any payment arrangements are handled there.
