About Caroline

A real person.
A personal approach.

Meet Caroline Murray, the person behind White Light Healing — a reflexologist, aromatherapist and holistic coach in Lucan.

Caroline Murray
WHY THIS WORK MATTERS TO ME

It begins
with listening.

Before holistic therapy, I spent more than 15 years in recruitment. I knew the demands of a busy career, long hours and travel. My own experience of stress led me to make a change.

That personal experience now informs how I work: making space for a conversation, treating each person as an individual, and taking time to listen.

My clinic is based in Lucan. I also bring wellbeing sessions to workplaces, drawing on my understanding of working life.

EXPERIENCE & APPROACH

Thoughtful care.
Clear expectations.

Training described by Caroline

Caroline’s website reports reflexology and aromatherapy training to ITEC standard, specialist reflexology training in fertility, maternity, cancer care, babies and children, and facial, hand and ear reflexology.

She also describes training as a Creative Mindfulness Practitioner for children and teenagers, and work with schools, parents and teachers.
