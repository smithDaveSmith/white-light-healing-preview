During foot reflexology, Caroline uses her thumbs and fingers to apply pressure across areas of the feet. Your first session includes time to discuss your wellbeing, preferences and any relevant health concerns.

Time for you,

Maternity reflexology offers time to rest during pregnancy. Caroline’s source website reports specialist maternity training; suitability should be discussed before you book.

04 / FERTILITY REFLEXOLOGY

A space to be heard.

60 minutes€75 · four-session option €280

For those navigating fertility, a session offers a private space for rest and personal attention. Caroline reports specialist training in this area.

05 / CHILDREN & TEENS

Care at their pace.

Child reflexology · 60 minutes€75

Caroline offers reflexology for children and teenagers, and her website describes additional training in children’s mindfulness.
