Preview privacy

Your information.
Handled simply.

This notice describes this unofficial concept. The business owner must supply and approve the privacy information for a future live website.

Contact form.

The draft form runs in your browser. This website does not submit your form entries to a message endpoint or save them in a site database. If you choose “Open email draft”, your selected mail application receives the text so that you can review and send it.

Do not put medical information into the draft form. Use a direct conversation with the practitioner for private care-related information.

Booking and external links.

The booking buttons open Caroline’s existing Acuity scheduling service. Appointment information, any payments and booking-provider cookies are handled by that service under its own notices. Google Maps and other external sites have their own privacy arrangements.

Opening preference.

This concept uses sessionStorage to remember that the opening sequence has played in this browser session. It stores no personal details. You can replay the opening from the homepage.

Technical requests.

Fonts, scripts and images are hosted with the concept. No analytics or marketing-cookie script has been added by this site. The hosting provider may process technical request information needed to operate the website.

Before a business launch.

The owner must confirm the data controller’s identity, purposes and legal bases, retention periods, processor arrangements and individual-rights contact process before adding a working submission endpoint, analytics or other data services. This preview notice is not a substitute for that business policy.

Contact.

For a care or appointment enquiry, contact Caroline at whitelighthealing@hotmail.com or 087 676 0725.
