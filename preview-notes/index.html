About this preview

An independent
design concept.

This website is an unofficial redesign proposal. It is not the business’s official website and does not imply approval by Caroline Murray.

What is connected.

Phone and email links use the publicly listed business contacts. Booking links lead to the existing White Light Healing Acuity service; appointments made there are real. This concept has no separate booking database, no payment checkout and no live message-submission endpoint.

Images and identity.

Caroline’s portrait and the voucher artwork come from the original website. Other photographs are marked as illustrative and are not presented as her real clients, treatments or clinic. The light-and-sun mark is a proposed interpretation of the original logo, not a claim of owner approval.

Facts and limitations.

The source website, public booking menu and directory evidence were reviewed on 15 October 2026. Qualifications are practitioner-reported. Current register membership, image permissions and live operating details need owner confirmation.

Visible content corrections.

  1. Stale date. Training plans: contact Caroline for current availability. Dates, fees and accreditation are not published. Evidence ↗
  2. Tariff scope. Reflexology from €75; publish exact source-backed tariffs per service. Describe 120-minute sessions only as enquiry subject to confirmation. Evidence ↗
  3. Unsupported physiological claims. Describe pressure techniques and intended relaxation experience. State complementary care is not medical diagnosis or a substitute for clinical care; make no fertility, hormone, immunity or disease-treatment promises. Evidence ↗
  4. Ear candling safety. Do not repeat safety or efficacy claims. Where preserving the service listing, disclose that evidence does not support ear-candling efficacy and there is risk of burns and ear damage; refer ear symptoms to a qualified clinician. Evidence ↗
  5. Reiki mechanism. Describe light touch or hands held above the body as a complementary practice chosen for a quiet, restful experience; no proven energy or health outcome claim. Evidence ↗
  6. Aftercare misinformation. Remove detox, energy-blockage and immune-strengthening explanations. Rest if helpful; drink normally to thirst; contact practitioner about concerns and seek appropriate medical advice for persistent/unexpected symptoms. Do not normalize a symptom as proof of benefit. Evidence ↗
  7. Unsupported statistic. Use the verified service logistics and relaxation purpose; omit statistic and guaranteed productivity/insurance outcomes. Ask visitors to check their own policy and current practitioner eligibility. Evidence ↗
  8. Placeholder publication. Exclude all placeholder content and redirect this route to homepage. No deletion executed. Evidence ↗
  9. Sitemap and legacy routes. Generate accurate route list. Redirect sample post and empty legacy shell to corrected blog/article or reflexology page; redirect historical foot-reflexology route to current foot section. Evidence ↗

Before this becomes official.

The owner needs to confirm the current service menu, prices, professional status, image and testimonial permissions, access details, training status and business privacy information. The preview remains marked noindex and is excluded from search crawling.

The full research record, design system, motion plan, route map and verification report accompany the source package.
